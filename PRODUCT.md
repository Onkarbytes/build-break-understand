# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro and TypeScript generate a static site for Cloudflare Pages. No CMS or runtime backend is part of this migration.

## Users

People looking to understand Onkar Tambake's engineering interests, work, research, writing, and ways to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

Provide a durable personal home for engineering, research, experimentation, and technical writing under the identity “BUILD. BREAK. UNDERSTAND.”

## Positioning

An independent engineer's record of building systems, investigating how they behave and fail, and documenting what is learned. This is not a corporate résumé.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Independent static routes exist for Home, About, Work, Research, Writing, Now, and Contact, with reusable project, investigation, and article detail routes.
- The site uses a shared Astro editorial shell and validated Markdown content collections. It builds to static files; there is no CMS, runtime backend, or administrator interface.
- No project outcomes, publications, employment history, certifications, contact details, or social profiles were supplied. Do not invent them.
- Content is intentionally separate from presentation in `src/content/` and `src/content.config.ts`; published entries require an explicit public flag and published status. The content model and future CMS boundary are documented in `CONTENT.md`.
- Appearance follows the operating system by default and can be toggled directly between light and night themes with one icon button; the preference persists.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Onkar Tambake.
- Technical identity: “BUILD. BREAK. UNDERSTAND.”
- Areas of interest supplied: cybersecurity and security engineering; AI and AI security; adversarial machine learning; agentic systems; software engineering and systems architecture; security research and technical experimentation; automation.
- Voice: curious, technically grounded, independent, and honest about what is and is not published.

## Evidence on Hand

The user-provided brief is the only source of biographical and professional facts. The project workspace was empty before implementation. No portfolio assets, case studies, writing, profile links, or contact address were supplied.

## Product Principles

- Make investigation and understanding as visible as building.
- Separate areas of interest from demonstrated work.
- Publish only verifiable details.
- Keep the site fast, accessible, and easy to maintain.
- Give each section a distinct reading or exploration rhythm while preserving the approved visual identity.
